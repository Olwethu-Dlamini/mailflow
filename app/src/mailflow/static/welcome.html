How it works

  1. 1

    Collect

    About every minute, MailFlow checks each connected mailbox (Gmail, your own domain, any IMAP host) and saves each new email.

  2. 2

    Sort

    An AI reads each email and sets the topic, who sent it, priority P1 to P3, whether it needs a reply, a one-line summary and the next step. Newsletters and no-reply mail are sorted by simple rules.

  3. 3

    Work the queue

    Everyone works one list: Needs reply, Mine, P1, Waiting on customer, Done and Everything. Search, or filter by mailbox, topic or sender. Take an email so nobody else answers it.

  4. 4

    Reply and close

    Reply from the page in the same conversation, then mark it Waiting on customer or Done. Made a mistake? Undo.

Built for a small team

  • Never answer twice

    Take an email and it is yours, listed under Mine. Admins can hand one to someone. Each email keeps a log of who took it, replied, closed or corrected it.

  • Correct the AI

    Wrong topic, priority or needs-reply? Anyone can fix it, and the email moves to the right place at once.

  • Nothing slips

    A P1 waiting over 4 hours, or a P2 over a day, turns red. A new P1 also sends a Telegram alert.

  • Keyboard first

    Keys 1 to 6 switch views, J and K move, A takes, E marks done, W waiting, Z undoes. Press ? to see them all.

  • On your phone

    The same queue works on a phone, with the sidebar as a drawer. Light or dark, as your device prefers.

  • Your own servers

    MailFlow and its AI (Mistral by default) run on the team's own server. Emails are kept 90 days, then cleaned up.

For admins: add accounts (email and password, or Continue with Google), connect mailboxes on the Integrations page, set up SMTP so any mailbox can send replies, and choose the AI provider and alerts.
