How MailFlow works

  1. Collect. About every minute, each connected mailbox is checked and new emails land here.
  2. Sort. An AI sets the topic, sender, priority P1–P3, whether it needs a reply, a summary and the next step.
  3. Work the queue. Switch views with 1–6, move with J K, and take an email with A so nobody else answers it.
  4. Reply and close. Reply in the same conversation, then W for waiting on the customer or E for done. Z undoes.

Tip: if the AI got the topic, priority or needs-reply wrong, change it on the email; it moves at once. Late emails turn red. Press ? for every key.
